# Sources and attribution

## Exact target and prior results

1. **Erik Koelink, Pablo Román, and Wadim Zudilin.** *A partial-sum deformation for a family of orthogonal polynomials.* arXiv:2409.00261, first submitted 30 August 2024, v2 dated 7 February 2025. Sections 2–3 give the recurrence and positivity assumptions; Conjecture 3.4 is the exact target. Proposition 3.1 proves eventual real simple roots and interlacing for large positive t. Section 4 discusses Hermite, Charlier, and Lommel examples. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00261) · [Verified v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.00261v2).
2. **The same authors and title.** Bibliographic publication: Indagationes Mathematicae 36 (2025), no. 6, 1745–1761. DOI [10.1016/j.indag.2025.04.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.indag.2025.04.007). [Publisher landing page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019357725000436). The final publisher full text was inaccessible, so the release makes no verified-identity claim between its statement and arXiv v2.

## Related subsequent research

3. **Antonio J. Durán.** *Zeros of linear combinations of orthogonal polynomials.* arXiv:2505.11956v1, submitted 17 May 2025. It studies fixed-length combinations of consecutive orthogonal polynomials as the degree varies, with eventual real-rootedness after suitable normalization. These are not the fixed-degree, fixed-normalization, varying-t quantifiers of the target. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11956) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11956v1).
4. **Antonio J. Durán.** *Zeros of linear combinations of Hermite polynomials.* arXiv:2505.15330v1, submitted 21 May 2025. It studies two Hermite normalizations and the effect of a coefficient polynomial. It does not provide the conjectured threshold for arbitrary orthogonal families. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15330) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15330v1).

## Distinct earlier problem

5. **Wadim Zudilin.** *On a family of polynomials related to ζ(2,1)=ζ(3).* arXiv:1504.07696 (2015; v2, 2017), later in Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 314 (2020), 621–630. This B-polynomial zero-location observation is a different problem and supplies neither the date nor the target of the present counterexample. [Source record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.07696).
